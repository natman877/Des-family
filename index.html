About DES Family Fest

DES Family Fest is more than just an event – it's a celebration of family bonds, joy, and togetherness. We believe every family deserves a day filled with laughter, connection, and unforgettable memories.

Founded with the vision of bringing families together through interactive experiences, we've created a unique space where kids, moms, dads, and the whole family can enjoy activities designed just for them.

Experience The Zones

🎠 Kids Zone

A vibrant, supervised play area filled with inflatables, interactive games, art corners, face painting, and mascot interactions.

Moms Zone

A calm and comfortable retreat designed just for moms: foot & hand massage, light beauty touch-ups, tea & refreshment corner.

Dads Zone

A fun, social zone where dads can unwind: pool & table tennis, mini football challenges, board games & relaxed seating.
